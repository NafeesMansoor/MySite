Privacy Policy – Bounce Rewind

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Bounce Rewind ("the game") is developed by Nafees Mansoor. This policy explains how the game handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests while you play.

Information stored on your device

Your high-score table (including any name you type for a high score) and your game settings are saved only on your own device so they are there next time you play. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Data in the game.

Permissions

The game requests no runtime permissions. It may use your device's vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings → Gameplay.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
